Privacy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The Perchlight app

Perchlight runs on your computer. We (the Perchlight project) don't run any server for the app and don't receive any of your data.

  • Your Cloudflare sign-in (an API token, or a "Sign in with Cloudflare" token) is stored encrypted on your computer with your operating system's key store, and is sent only to Cloudflare.
  • Your projects and screens are stored only on your computer.
  • Your data (KV values, D1 rows, mailing-list subscribers) goes directly between your computer and Cloudflare's API. It never passes through us.
  • The app has no analytics, tracking or crash reporting.

When you use "Sign in with Cloudflare", Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to that sign-in. Perchlight asks only for the permissions it needs: reading your account details, and editing Workers KV and D1.

This website

This website collects no personal information. Update notices come through GitHub's own "Watch" feature, under GitHub's privacy policy.

This website uses no cookies and no analytics.
