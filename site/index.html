Thirteen tools, grouped by the job they do

Each one is its own repository with a setup guide. Use one, or all of them on the same account.

Ship and sell your app

perchlight-updates

An update server for Electron and Tauri apps: release channels, gradual rollouts, pulling a bad release in one command, and private links for testers.

Replaces paid update and download hosting

perchlight-crashes

Crash and error reports for desktop apps and games. Electron's crash reporter and Sentry's SDKs send to it unchanged, with one alert per new issue or regression.

Replaces a crash-reporting subscription

perchlight-keys

License keys with signed passes your app checks offline, device limits, and keys issued from Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad or itch.io.

Replaces paid license key services

perchlight-beta

A private beta and advance-reader portal: applications, invites, NDA records, personal download links, feedback and bug reports.

Replaces paid beta testing portals

perchlight-play

Leaderboards, cloud saves and remote config for games, with stated anti-cheat limits and saves that are never silently overwritten.

Replaces paid game backend services

Hear from people

perchlight-list

A mailing list: double opt-in signups, spam protection, and announcements through Resend, Amazon SES, Mailgun, Postmark or Cloudflare Email.

Replaces a monthly newsletter-service bill

perchlight-forms

A backend for your website's forms, from plain HTML: contact, signups and file uploads, a spam folder, and an email or webhook for each submission.

Replaces a paid form service

perchlight-booking

A "pick a time with me" page that reads your busy times from your calendar's private link, never double-books, and sends invites with cancel and reschedule links.

Replaces a scheduling-page subscription

perchlight-ask

Answers questions from your own docs with links to the pages it used, and drafts support replies for you to approve. It never sends on its own.

Replaces paid docs chatbots and AI help desks

Keep it running

perchlight-guard

Warns you at 80% and 100% of every Cloudflare free-plan limit, and of GitHub Actions' free allowance, before you hit errors or a bill.

Replaces finding out from an error page or an invoice

perchlight-status

Uptime checks from two regions, a public status page with incidents and 90-day history, and warnings before domains and certificates expire.

Replaces paid uptime monitors and status pages

perchlight-hooks

A webhook inspector with signature checks, replay and forwarding, scheduled jobs that call your URLs, and heartbeat monitors for jobs that stop checking in.

Replaces paid webhook, cron and heartbeat services

perchlight-privacy

Checks the websites you own for trackers, cookies set before consent, missing privacy headers and insecure forms, and alerts you when something changes.

Replaces paid website privacy scanners

One window for every site you run

The desktop app keeps a project per site or app, each with its own Cloudflare account, and shows your data as screens you design.

Screens without code
Pick a D1 table or a set of KV keys. Perchlight samples the records and suggests columns; you choose what's shown, what's editable, and what each row's buttons do.
Or write them yourself
Screens are a perchlight.json file with a published schema, so you or your AI agent can write one. Imports are checked and every problem is explained.
A screen for every tool
Each self-hosted tool ships a screen file: subscribers and announcements, releases and rollouts, usage and alerts.
Your data stays between you and Cloudflare
Your sign-in is stored with your operating system's key store and sent only to Cloudflare. The app has no analytics.
