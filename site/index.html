A free desktop app for your Cloudflare data

Browse and edit Workers KV and D1, build your own screens over your data without writing code, and run a mailing list, for every site you run.

Download for Windows, macOS or Linux View the source

Free and open source (MIT). Installers aren't code-signed, so your system will ask you to confirm once. How to open them.

One app, all your sites

A project per site or app, each with its own Cloudflare account. Switch in one click.

Screens without code

Pick a table or a set of keys. Perchlight suggests the columns, and you choose what's shown, editable, and what each row's buttons do.

Or write them yourself

Screens are a perchlight.json file with a published schema, so you or your AI agent can write it. Imports are checked and every problem is explained.

A mailing list included

Pairs with perchlight-list, a free self-hosted mailing list on Cloudflare: double opt-in, spam protection, and announcements through Resend, Amazon SES, Mailgun, Postmark or Cloudflare Email.

Get updates

A short email when there's a new version. Unsubscribe any time. (This form runs on perchlight-list.)
